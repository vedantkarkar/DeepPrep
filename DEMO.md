# DeepPrep — 3–5 Minute Hackathon Judge Presentation Script

## 1. Problem Introduction (00:00 – 00:30)
> *"Judges, engineering candidates spend months applying to jobs with generic resumes without knowing if their preparation actually matches what a specific company requires. Keyword scanners falsely claim they are ready, leading to rejected applications. DeepPrep fixes this by turning resume claims into verified evidence, evaluating deterministic role readiness, and generating a personalized study roadmap."*

---
